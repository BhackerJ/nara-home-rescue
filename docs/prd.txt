Part 2 — Product Requirements Document
Version 1.0 · October 25, 2026 · Target demo: October 31, 2026
1. Product overview
Product name: Nara Home Rescue

Product type: Persistent multi-agent home-service orchestration prototype.

Primary user: Homeowner experiencing an urgent HVAC issue.

Secondary user: Simulated HVAC service provider.

Core hypothesis: A persistent home-service actor coordinating specialized AI agents can maintain context and complete a multi-step service workflow despite interruptions and changing availability.

Hackathon objective: Deliver a technically credible, visually clear, end-to-end demonstration of state persistence, agent collaboration and service resolution.

Business objective: Explore reusable architecture for Nara's Home Twin and future service coordination layer.

6. Core data contracts

type CaseStatus =
  | "created"
  | "diagnosing"
  | "dispatching"
  | "awaiting_provider"
  | "recovering"
  | "negotiating"
  | "confirmed"
  | "failed";

interface ServiceCase {
  id: string;
  homeId: string;
  equipmentId: string;
  issue: string;
  symptoms: string[];
  status: CaseStatus;
  checkpoint: string;
  revision: number;
  requestedSlot?: string;
  confirmedSlot?: string;
  providerId?: string;
  events: CaseEvent[];
}

interface CaseEvent {
  id: string;
  caseId: string;
  timestamp: string;
  agent: string;
  type: string;
  payload: Record<string, unknown>;
}



Every meaningful transition should be persisted before advancing the workflow. On recovery, the actor should identify the last completed step and safely resume unfinished work. Use idempotency keys for provider requests to prevent duplicate bookings after retries.
